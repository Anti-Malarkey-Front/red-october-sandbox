# Credits

## The game

- **Red October: Fall of Empire** is by Eva, 2026: the setting, the history, the events, the parties, and the systems added for 1917-1921.
- It is a fork of **[Social Democracy: An Alternate History](https://github.com/aucchen/social_democracy_alternate_history)** by **Autumn Chen** (2024). The engine integration, the interface, and the structure of the cards, advisors and elections are her work.
- **[Dendry](https://github.com/idmillington/dendry)**, the interactive-fiction engine, is by **Ian Millington**; the game runs on Autumn Chen's fork, **[dendrynexus](https://github.com/aucchen/dendrynexus)**.

Both the original game and this fork are released under the [MIT licence](LICENSE).

## Libraries

- [jQuery](https://jquery.com) 1.11.1 (MIT licence).
- [d3.js](https://d3js.org) v7 (ISC licence), with [d3-parliament](https://github.com/geoffreybr/d3-parliament) by Geoffrey Brossard (MIT licence), which draws the parliament charts.

## Pictures and music

The original game's images and music are not included in this fork. The period photographs, posters and recordings in Red October are in the public domain and come from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org):

- portraits of 26 of the advisors and pictures for 24 events, 1917-1921;
- four recordings: the Workers' Marseillaise (1917), Varshavianka (1928), Sergei Rachmaninoff playing his Prelude in C-sharp minor (1920) and the Internationale.

Every file is listed, with its author, title, date, source page and licence, in [docs/credits/images.txt](docs/credits/images.txt) and [docs/credits/music.txt](docs/credits/music.txt), and on the Credits pages inside the game. The card art, title banner and party emblems are generated by the scripts in `tools/build/`.
